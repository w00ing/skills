# Chinese Humanization Checklist

Use this checklist after reading the source copy. The goal is natural Mainland Chinese public-facing writing, not decorative rewriting.

## Translationese Patterns

Fix literal English structures:

- `通过……来……` overuse.
- `旨在帮助用户……` when a direct verb is clearer.
- `无缝地`, `高效地`, `轻松地` stacked without proof.
- `不仅……而且……`, `不只是……更是……` used as a default contrast.
- `为……提供了一个……的解决方案`.
- `使得用户能够……`.
- `在……方面` when a concrete verb would do.
- `满足您的……需求` when the need can be named directly.

Prefer direct phrasing grounded in source material, such as:

- `应用会按设定时间运行。`
- `可以将文件导出为 PDF。`
- `更改会自动保存。`

## AI-Sounding Public Copy

Flag and rewrite:

- inflated adjectives: `强大`, `极致`, `卓越`, `革命性`, `领先`, `专业级`
- generic outcomes: `提升效率`, `提高生产力`, `优化工作流`
- slogan filler: `一站式`, `全方位`, `智能化`, `赋能`, `打造`
- vague trust claims: `安全可靠`, `保护隐私`, `值得信赖` without mechanism
- repeated triplets: `快速、简单、高效`, `清晰、稳定、可靠`

Replace with specific, verifiable details supported by source material:

- `最近 7 天的数据会显示在报告中。`
- `更改会自动保存。`
- `可以按需下载为 PDF。`

## SEO Stuffing

Chinese SEO terms can appear, but prose must still read like copy for people.

Use relevant target terms naturally:

- `中文校对工具`
- `中文润色服务`
- `项目管理软件`
- `PDF 转换工具`

Avoid heading/title bundles:

- `中文校对工具｜中文润色服务｜写作辅助工具`
- `项目管理软件、团队协作工具、任务跟踪平台`

## Product And Service Claims

Before finalizing, check every claim:

- Are features, platform support, and integrations consistent with current source material?
- Are privacy, security, or data-handling claims supported by that material?
- Are pricing, availability, legal, or compliance claims current and specific?
- Does the copy imply a guarantee or capability that the source does not establish?

If uncertain, make the claim narrower.

## Mainland Chinese Tone

Prefer:

- practical, calm, specific language
- verbs over nouns
- user workflow before product praise
- short paragraphs
- natural CTAs

Avoid:

- Taiwan/Hong Kong vocabulary
- internet slang
- overly cute tone
- official-bureaucratic phrasing unless legal/support copy requires it
- planning notes, implementation jargon, or internal marketing language in reader-facing copy

## CTA Patterns

Good CTA directions:

- `了解产品`
- `查看功能`
- `查看价格`
- `开始使用`
- `了解详情`

Avoid vague CTAs:

- `立即体验`
- `探索更多`
- `开启高效之旅`
- `解锁强大功能`

## Self-Check

Before returning final copy:

1. Read it aloud in Chinese.
2. Check that every sentence could plausibly appear on a Mainland Chinese product site.
3. Remove one filler adjective if the sentence still works.
4. Confirm Chinese-English spacing and punctuation.
5. Check product and service claims against current source material.
6. Keep internal planning language out of reader-facing copy.
